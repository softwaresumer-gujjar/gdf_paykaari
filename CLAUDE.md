# Milk Collection and Distribution Business — Source Document

## Overview

This business involves collecting milk from multiple farmers, transporting it, and supplying it to retail shops across the city.

---

## Milk Collection Process

Milk is collected from different farmers and loaded onto vehicles. Before collection, milk quality is tested:

- Fat content
- Presence of added water
- Milk density

---

## Transportation Monitoring

During transportation, the following are tracked:

- Travel time from farm to retail
- Stops made during the journey

---

## Storage and Handling

Milk is stored and transported in tanks.

Example:
- Total collection: 1,000 liters
- Each tank: 40 liters (1 maund)
- Total tanks: 40

The same tanks collected from farmers are delivered to retailers.

---

## Farmer Agreements

Farmers enter **yearly contracts** with fixed commitments.

Example:
- Daily supply: 10 maunds (1 maund = 40 liters)
- Total: 400 liters per day
- Fixed rate: 8,000 PKR per maund

### Excess Supply
If a farmer supplies **more** than the agreed quantity:
- Extra milk is paid at the **open market rate**
- Market rate is set **twice daily** (morning and evening)

Example:
- Market rate: 9,000 PKR/maund → extra milk paid at 9,000 PKR/maund

### Short Supply
If a farmer supplies **less** than agreed:
- Supplied quantity is paid at the fixed rate
- Shortfall is charged at market rate (loss recovered from farmer)

Example:
- Committed: 10 maunds | Supplied: 9 maunds
- Market rate: 9,000 PKR | Fixed rate: 8,000 PKR
- Loss: 1,000 PKR recovered from the farmer

---

## Retailer Agreements

Retailers also have **fixed agreements**.

Example:
- Daily purchase: 4 maunds
- Fixed rate: 10,000 PKR per maund

### Excess Purchase
If a retailer buys **more** than committed:
- Charged at market rate + 300 PKR transport surcharge

Example:
- Market rate: 12,000 PKR → Final rate: 12,300 PKR/maund

---

## Communication

Farmers receive:
- Milk quantity and quality reports
- Billing details

Channels:
- WhatsApp
- Email
- SMS

---

## Staff Management

Employees:
- 1 driver
- 2–3 helpers

Their salaries are tracked and managed as part of business operations.
